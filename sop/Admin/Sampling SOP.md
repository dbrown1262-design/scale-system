# Sampling SOP

## Section 1 — Purpose

The purpose of this SOP is to prepare product and documentation for a scheduled sampling appointment with the laboratory sampler. The sampler will select flower and/or pre-roll samples for laboratory analysis.

## Section 2 — Scope

This procedure applies to all sampling events conducted for laboratory compliance testing.

## Section 3 — Responsibilities

### Processing Staff
- Prepare all required documentation
- Retrieve METRC tags
- Retrieve product for sampling
- Set up video recording system
- Assist sampler as needed

### Verification Staff
- Verify METRC tag numbers match the Compliance Analysis Request (CAR) forms

## Section 4 — Required Materials

- Compliance Analysis Request (CAR) forms
- Transportation Manifest
- METRC package tags
- Product buckets (flower) or bins (pre-rolls)
- Computer with access to Synology Surveillance Station

## Section 5 — Procedure

### 5.1 Documentation Preparation

1. Prepare two Compliance Analysis Request (CAR) forms for each requested sample.

2. Prepare two Transportation Manifests listing all sample batches.

### 5.2 METRC Tag Verification

1. For each CAR form:
   - Retrieve the METRC tag for the METRC Package Number
   - Place the tag with the corresponding CAR form.
   - A second employee shall verify that the tag numbers match the CAR form

### 5.3 Product Preparation

1. Retrieve the product associated with each sample:
   - Flower: retrieve the corresponding bucket(s)
   - Pre-rolls: retrieve the corresponding bin(s)

2. Place the product on the sampling table next to the corresponding CAR form.

### 5.4 Video Recording Procedure

1. On the computer in the crew room:
   - Open Google Chrome
   - Select the bookmark:
     **"Synology Surveillance Station - HempHouse"**

2. Sign in using the assigned credentials.
   - User Id:  AdkAdmin
   - Password: Adk1891?

3. When the main screen appears:
   - Select the **IP Camera** icon
   - Locate and select the camera:
     **"Processing - Sampling"**

4. Verify the sampling table is visible on screen.

5. When the sampler begins the sampling process:
   - Click the **Manual Recording** icon located in the lower left corner of the screen

6. Verify that:
   - A red recording bar appears at the top of the screen
   - Recording has started successfully

7. At the end of the sampling session:
   - Click the **Stop** icon in the red recording bar
   - Verify recording has stopped

### 5.5 End of Session

At the end of the session, the sampler should sign both Transportation Manifests.  Retain the second copy of the manifest and the CARs for our records.

## Section 6 — Records

The following records shall be retained:
- Compliance Analysis Request (CAR) forms
- Transportation Manifest
- Sampling video recordings
- Associated METRC records
