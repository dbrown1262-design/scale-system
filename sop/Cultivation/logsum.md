# Cultivation Logs Index (GMP + METRC Integrated)

## Purpose

This document defines all required cultivation logs and establishes the relationship between internal records and the METRC system of record.

All logs are designed to:

* Avoid duplication with METRC
* Provide evidence of process control (GMP)
* Support audit readiness and traceability

---

## Section 1 — System of Record

### 1.1 METRC Responsibilities

The METRC system serves as the official record for:

* Plant inventory and lifecycle tracking
* Harvest batches and weights
* Package creation and transfers
* Waste destruction records

No duplicate manual logs shall be maintained for these functions.

---

### 1.2 Internal Logs Responsibilities

Internal logs are required to document:

* Environmental conditions
* Cultivation processes and inputs
* Equipment use and sanitation
* Personnel actions
* Deviations and corrective actions
* Verification of METRC accuracy

---

## Section 2 — Required Logs

### 2.1 Environmental Monitoring Logs

**Purpose:** Document controlled environmental conditions

* Grow Room Environmental Log
* Clone/Nursery Environmental Log

**Key Data:**

* Temperature (°F)
* Relative Humidity (%)
* VPD (if applicable)
* Date / Time
* Initials

**Frequency:** Minimum 1× per day per room

---

### 2.2 Irrigation / Feeding Log

**Purpose:** Document nutrient and irrigation inputs

**Key Data:**

* Date / Time
* Room
* Strain / Batch
* Nutrient recipe
* EC / pH
* Volume applied
* Initials

---

### 2.3 Stock Tank Log

**Purpose:** Record preparation of nutrient solutions

**Key Data:**

* Date
* Tank ID
* Ingredients and quantities
* EC / pH verification
* Prepared by / verified by

---

### 2.4 Integrated Pest Management (IPM) Logs

#### IPM Monitoring Log

**Purpose:** Record pest/disease observations

* Date
* Room
* Findings
* Action required (Y/N)
* Initials

#### IPM Application Log

**Purpose:** Record all treatments applied

* Date
* Product name
* EPA registration (if applicable)
* Rate / method
* Target pest
* Applicator initials

---

### 2.5 Sanitation Log

**Purpose:** Document cleaning and sanitation activities

* Date
* Area / equipment cleaned
* Cleaning agent used
* Method
* Initials
* Verification (if required)

---

### 2.6 Clone Batch Log

**Purpose:** Track propagation process (NOT inventory)

**Key Data:**

* Date
* Strain
* Source plants
* Number of cuttings taken
* Rooting method
* Environmental conditions
* Success rate (if tracked)
* **METRC Plant Batch ID (created)**

---

### 2.7 Transplant Log

**Purpose:** Document plant movement and process conditions

**Key Data:**

* Date
* From stage → To stage
* Strain
* Batch reference
* Media used
* Additives / beneficials
* Initials
* **METRC Plant Batch ID**

*Note: Plant counts are maintained in METRC and are not duplicated here.*

---

### 2.8 Cannabis Waste Disposal Log

**Purpose:** Document waste handling (supporting record to METRC)

**Key Data:**

* Date
* Material type
* Reason for disposal
* Method of destruction
* Initials
* **METRC Package ID**
* **METRC Waste Record Confirmation**

---

### 2.9 METRC Verification Log

**Purpose:** Verify accuracy of METRC records

**Frequency:** Minimum weekly

**Procedure:**

* Select random sample of plants/packages
* Verify:

  * Tag present and readable
  * Tag matches METRC entry
  * Physical count matches system

**Key Data:**

* Date
* Area checked
* Number of items verified
* Discrepancies (if any)
* Corrective action
* Initials

---

### 2.10 Training Log

**Purpose:** Document employee training and SOP review

**Key Data:**

* Employee name
* SOP name/version
* Training date
* Trainer
* Employee signature/initials

---

### 2.11 Deviation & Corrective Action (CAPA) Log

**Purpose:** Centralized tracking of all deviations

**Examples:**

* Environmental excursions
* Equipment failures
* METRC discrepancies
* Process errors

**Key Data:**

* Date
* Description of deviation
* Affected area/batch
* Immediate action taken
* Root cause (if known)
* Corrective action
* Preventive action
* Verification of closure
* Initials

---

## Section 3 — Records Management

### 3.1 Completion Requirements

* All entries must be made in real time
* No blank fields (use “N/A” if not applicable)
* Corrections must be:

  * Single line strike-through
  * Initialed and dated

---

### 3.2 Retention

All logs shall be retained per New York State Office of Cannabis Management requirements (minimum 5 years unless otherwise specified).

---

### 3.3 Format

Logs may be:

* Paper-based (printed templates), or
* Electronic (validated system such as Supabase-based applications)

Electronic systems must:

* Maintain audit trails
* Prevent unauthorized edits
* Be backed up regularly

---

## Section 4 — Guiding Principle

METRC documents **what happened**.
Internal logs document **how and under what conditions it happened**.

Both are required for full GMP compliance.
