# Cultivation — Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

**Function:** Cultivation
**Role:** Head Grower
**Applies To:** Nursery, Propagation, Transplanting, Grow Rooms, IPM, Harvest Prep, Sanitation, Recordkeeping

## Section 1 — Purpose

To define standardized, controlled procedures for cannabis cultivation activities from propagation through harvest preparation, ensuring consistency, product quality, and compliance with New York State OCM regulations and GMP expectations.

## Section 2 — Responsibilities

The Head Grower is responsible for:

- Nursery and mother plant maintenance
- Propagation and transplanting execution
- Environmental control and monitoring
- Watering, feeding, and nutrient management
- Pest and disease monitoring and IPM application
- Harvest preparation and plant tracking
- Data collection and recordkeeping
- Sanitation and contamination prevention

All cultivation staff must follow this SOP and report deviations immediately.

## Section 3 — Required Equipment & Supplies

- Pruning scissors / trimming shears
- Isopropyl alcohol (70%) or approved sanitizer
- Disposable gloves
- Sticky pest traps
- IPM spray solutions
- Clone trays and humidity domes
- Coco coir medium and pots
- Batch tanks and mixing equipment
- EC / pH meters
- Measuring containers
- Sprayers (IPM and watering)
- Portable lighting
- PPFD meter
- Infrared thermometer
- Temperature / humidity meters
- CO₂ monitoring system
- Beneficial additives (e.g., Azos, Recharge, nematodes)
- Shoe sanitation mats
- Waste containers

## Section 4 — Plant Care Procedures

### 4.1 Nursery Plant Maintenance

1. Inspect mother plants daily for:
   - Nutrient deficiencies
   - Pest or disease presence
2. Perform pruning, topping, and defoliation as required
3. Sanitize tools between plants
4. Maintain mother plant lifecycle (replacement as needed)
5. Execute IPM schedule
6. Record all activities in Nursery Log

### 4.2 Grow Room Maintenance

1. Perform scheduled:
   - Topping
   - Defoliation
   - Trellising
2. Monitor plant health daily
3. Maintain environmental parameters:
   - Temperature
   - Relative Humidity
   - VPD
   - CO₂
   - PPFD
4. Maintain pest traps and monitoring
5. Report abnormalities immediately

## Section 5 — Propagation & Transplanting

### 5.1 Propagation (Cloning)

1. Prepare sanitized clone trays and tools
2. Take clones per Cloning SOP
3. Place under humidity domes and label with:
   - Strain
   - Date
4. Monitor daily for:
   - Root development
   - Mold or stress
5. Maintain environmental conditions appropriate for propagation

### 5.2 Transplant Preparation (Room & Medium)

#### Room Preparation (24–48 Hours Prior)

- Set temperature: 70–80°F
- Set humidity: 70–80% (maintain 1–3 days post-transplant)
- VPD: 0.4–0.8 kPa
- Lighting:
  - 18/6 cycle
  - 200–500 PPFD
  - ~36” above canopy
- Stage coco coir bricks:
  - ~108 per table (12 per zone)

#### Medium Preparation (Coco Wet-Up)

1. Fill batch tank (~100 gallons)
2. Adjust:
   - pH: ~6.2
   - EC: 1.1–1.3
3. Apply 2 emitters per brick
4. Repeat until field capacity achieved
5. Medium must be moist but not saturated

#### Pot Preparation

1. Remove emitters
2. Dig transplant holes:
   - Depth: approx. hand depth
   - Avoid damaging pots

### 5.3 Transplant Execution

1. Sanitize work surfaces and tools
2. Apply beneficials (optional):
   - Azos at ~1 tbsp per hole
3. Stage clone racks
4. Label each zone/table with strain

#### Transplant Process

1. Move systematically table-by-table
2. Select strongest rooted clones first
3. Place clone in hole while supporting the stem
4. Backfill with coco:
   - Firm but not compacted
5. Lightly secure surface

### 5.4 Post-Transplant Procedures

#### Recharge Watering

1. Prepare batch tank:
   - EC: 0.8–1.1
   - pH: 5.5–5.9
2. Sanitize barrels and tools
3. Mix:
   - Recharge (~75 mg per barrel)
   - Optional nematodes
4. Hand water around root zone

#### Environmental Stabilization

- Maintain high humidity (70–80%) for 1–3 days
- Keep light intensity reduced

#### Feeding Schedule

- Do not water for 1–7 days unless needed
- Resume watering when pots are light
- Begin nutrients after 3–5 days

#### Plant Training

- Perform topping 3–7 days post-transplant once growth resumes

#### Sprays

- Delay IPM and foliar sprays 3–7 days post-transplant

## Section 6 — Watering, Feeding & Stock Tank Mixing

1. Perform scheduled:
   - Feeding
   - Recharge watering
   - Flushing
2. Verify EC and pH before application
3. Record all feed data

### Stock Tank Mixing

1. Prepare nutrient solutions per formula
2. Verify EC/pH
3. Label tanks:
   - Date
   - Formula
4. Clean tanks between uses

## Section 7 — Pest & Disease Management

### Monitoring

- Inspect plants regularly
- Check sticky traps weekly
- Replace traps every 7 days

### IPM Application

1. Prepare approved solutions
2. Set room:
   - Lights off
   - Temp < 80°F
3. Apply sprays thoroughly
4. Record:
   - Product
   - Date
   - Room
   - Applicator

## Section 8 — Harvest Support

### Plant Labeling

- Verify METRC tags present and legible
- Replace missing/damaged tags immediately

### Harvest Handling

- Maintain strain segregation
- Transport carefully
- Report discrepancies

## Section 9 — Recordkeeping

The following must be documented:

- Plant inventory
- Environmental conditions
- Feeding and irrigation data
- IPM applications
- Transplant and propagation events

All records must be:

- Accurate
- Timely
- Traceable
- Maintained per compliance requirements

## Section 10 — Sanitation

### General Requirements

- Maintain clean work environment at all times
- Prevent cross-contamination

### Tool & Equipment Cleaning

- Sanitize all reusable equipment
- Air dry before storage

### Post-Harvest Sanitation

1. Remove plant material
2. Clean all surfaces
3. Apply disinfectant
4. Allow full drying

### Shoe Mat Maintenance

- Inspect and refill daily

### Water System Sanitation

- Clean sediment filters regularly

### Cannabis Waste

- Render unusable
- Log disposal per regulations

## Section 11 — Compliance & Deviations

- All deviations must be reported immediately
- Document corrective actions
- Maintain readiness for audit at all times
- Follow all NYS OCM and internal SOP requirements
