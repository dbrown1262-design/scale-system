# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
## Sampling of Cannabis Batches for Laboratory Testing

---

## Section 1 — Purpose

To define standardized procedures for collecting representative cannabis samples from production batches for laboratory testing in compliance with New York State Office of Cannabis Management (OCM) regulations and METRC tracking requirements.

---

## Section 2 — Scope

This SOP applies to all cannabis flower batches designated for laboratory testing prior to transfer or sale.

---

## Section 3 — Responsibilities

### 3.1 Sampling Personnel
- Samples are selected by the representative of Kaycha Labs
- Must follow this SOP exactly
- Must ensure accurate documentation in METRC and internal systems


---

## Section 4 — Definitions

- **Batch**: A defined quantity of cannabis product identified by a unique METRC package ID
- **Sample**: A representative portion collected for laboratory testing
- **Chain of Custody**: Documentation tracking sample from collection to lab transfer

---

## Section 5 — Materials and Equipment

- Clean gloves (nitrile)
- Sampling tools (sanitized scissors or forceps)
- Sample containers (lab-approved)
- Labels (METRC UID + internal label if applicable)
- Scale (calibrated)
- Cleaning supplies (isopropyl alcohol)

---

## Section 6 — Pre-Sampling Requirements

### 6.1 Batch Verification
- Confirm METRC package ID
- Confirm strain and batch details
- Verify batch is ready for testing

### 6.2 Area Preparation
- Sampling area must be clean and free of contaminants
- No food or drink present
- Tools must be sanitized before use

### 6.3 Video Surveillance
- Confirm cameras are operational and recording
- Sampling must occur within camera view when required

---

## Section 7 — Sampling Procedure

### 7.1 General Requirements
- Wear gloves at all times
- Use clean tools
- Avoid contamination or bias in selection

### 7.2 Sample Collection Method - Eights Jars

1. Identify the batch and confirm METRC package ID
2. Provide the required number of jars for sample
3. Kaycha Representative will randomly select flower material and place the appropriate amount (3.5 grams) in each jar

### 7.3 Handling
- Place sample into approved container immediately
- Minimize handling time
- Do not compress or damage material

---

## Section 8 — Labeling

Each sample must include:

- METRC Sample Package ID (generated in METRC)
- Source Batch/Package ID
- Strain name
- Date of sampling
- Initials of sampler

Internal labels may include:
- Internal batch UID
- Container/bucket reference

---

## Section 9 — METRC Entry

1. Create a new sample package in METRC
2. Assign correct source package
3. Record weight of sample
4. Designate as "Test Sample"
5. Record sampling date

---

## Section 10 — Documentation

The following must be recorded:

- Date and time of sampling
- Employee performing sampling
- Source batch/package ID
- Sample package ID
- Sample weight
- Method of sampling

Records must be stored in:
- METRC
- Internal tracking system (e.g., batch log or database)

---

## Section 11 — Chain of Custody

- Sample must remain secured after collection
- Store in designated area until transfer
- Record transfer to laboratory including:
  - Date and time
  - Receiving party
  - Transport method

---

## Section 12 — Post-Sampling

- Return remaining product to storage
- Clean and sanitize tools
- Remove gloves and dispose properly
- Verify all entries in METRC and internal system

---

## Section 13 — Deviations

Any deviation from this SOP must be documented, including:

- Description of deviation
- Reason
- Corrective action taken

---

## Section 14 — Corrective Actions

If sampling errors or issues occur:

- Notify Compliance Manager immediately
- Evaluate need for re-sampling
- Document incident and resolution

---

## Section 15 — Record Retention

All sampling records must be retained in accordance with OCM requirements (minimum 5 years or as required).
