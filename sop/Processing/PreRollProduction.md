# PreRoll Production — SOP

**Function:** Processing & Packaging
**Process:** PreRoll Grinding, Manufacturing, and Packaging
**Applies To:** Material Preparation, Grinding, Cone Filling, PreRoll Production, Quality Control, and Packaging


## 1. Overview

This SOP defines the complete workflow for producing and packaging compliant cannabis pre-rolls, from grinding "smalls" material through final packaging. The procedure ensures consistent quality, proper weights, and repeatable results across all production stages.

## 2. Responsibilities

- **Processing Technician:** Execute grinding, production, and packaging steps
- **Processing Lead:** Verify batch weights, quality, and process compliance
- **QA / Compliance:** Ensure sanitation and traceability requirements are met

## 3. Safety & Sanitation (All Stages)

**Required PPE:**
- Gloves
- Hairnet
- Beard net/mask

**Sanitation:**
- Clean all work areas, machines, tools, and bins with isopropyl alcohol before starting
- Sanitize surfaces before and after production
- Prevent cross-strain contamination
- Dispose of waste material per facility SOP

## PART A: GRINDING

### Purpose
Ensuring only quality "smalls" material is processed for prerolls.

### Required Tools & Equipment
- "Smalls" bags
- Grinder (with 3 grind pieces: wide, medium, small)
- Storage containers
- Sorting bin

### Procedure

1. **Preparation**
   - Clean area, tools, and bins with isopropyl alcohol
   - Assemble grinder with widest grind piece first

2. **Sorting**
   - Sort smalls in sorting bin
   - Separate any extra stems and leaf material

3. **First Grind (Wide)**
   - Run all sorted products through grinder into storage containers

4. **Second Grind (Medium)**
   - Disassemble and switch to medium grind piece
   - Repeat grind

5. **Final Grind (Fine)**
   - Disassemble and switch to smallest grind piece
   - Complete final grind

6. **Storage**
   - Label containers with strain and harvest date
   - Store properly

## PART B: PREROLL PRODUCTION

### Purpose
Utilizing ground material to produce quality prerolls using the PreRoll machine system.

### Required Equipment & Components

**Machine Components:**
- PreRoll machine
- Grates (to bounce cones)
- Collectors
- Cone tray and holder
- Press
- Star tray and 109mm closing tray

**Dispensing System Components:**
- Dispensing tray
- Top alignment tray
- 2 × corner pins
- Collection guard

**Tools & Materials:**
- PreRoll cones
- Ground material
- Digital scale
- Star tamper
- Sliding cone holder
- Pegs for finished pre-roll transfer
- Storage containers for finished product

### Procedure

#### 1. Setup & Assembly

1. Clean area, machine, and parts with isopropyl alcohol
2. Assemble preroll machine (**Don't forget the grate**)
3. Verify all components are properly seated and aligned

#### 2. Cone Tray Loading

1. Add cones to agitator
2. Leave **2 corners empty** to account for pins for the dispensing tray
3. Inspect cones for damage or deformities before loading

#### 3. Assemble Dispensing Tray using:

- Top alignment tray
- 2 corner pins
- Collection guard

Verify proper alignment and stable stacking

#### 4. Weighing & Loading Material

1. **Tare** dispensing tray on scale
2. Add **at least 60 grams of material per load**
3. Prepare **3 loads per batch**
4. Set tray onto the machine and dispense

#### 5. Packing Operation

1. Set machine speed/control to **18–21**
2. Let material pack for **at least 5 minutes per load**
3. Use longer time for fluffy material
4. Visually verify uniform packing during operation

#### 6. Pressing & Closing

1. Once all **3 loads are packed**, put cone tray into press
2. Use sliding holder to position cones into closing tray
3. Press into closing tray
4. Apply **star tamper** using the following sequence:
   - Press onto paper, use star side to close
   - Use peg side
   - Use star side again
   - Finish with peg side one more time

#### 7. Storage

1. Label containers with strain name and harvest date
2. Store finished prerolls properly

## PART C: PACKAGING

### Purpose
Ensuring only quality prerolls are packaged for sale.

### Required Tools & Materials
- Scale
- Preroll tubes
- Preroll caps
- Hydro packs
- Rubber bands
- Labels:
  - Primary labels (strain specific)
  - Secondary labels
  - Tamper seals
  - Metrc labels (for harvests prior to 2/16/26)
- Finished prerolls

### Procedure

#### 1. Quality Control & Weighing

1. **Scale each preroll** to ensure proper weight:
   - **1.3+ grams without tare**
   - **1.0+ grams with tare**
2. Check each preroll for:
   - Wrinkles
   - Holes
   - Imperfections
3. Set aside any prerolls that don't meet standards

#### 2. Tube Preparation & Loading

1. Label preroll tubes with strain-specific **primary label**
2. Add one preroll to each tube
3. Add one hydro pack to each tube
4. Cap off each used preroll tube

#### 3. Final Labeling & Sealing

1. Add **secondary label** to designated gray area on primary
2. Add **tamper seal** from cap down along primary seam

#### 4. Quality Check & Bundling

1. Perform final quality check on packaging
2. Bundle prerolls in groups of **10** with rubber band
3. Store properly

## Quality Control Standards

- Verify cone count per batch
- Verify batch weight totals (minimum 180g ground material per batch)
- Verify individual preroll weights (1.3g+ without tare, 1.0g+ with tare)
- Inspect closure quality
- Check for wrinkles, holes, and imperfections
- Remove defective pre-rolls
- Document rework or waste

## Compliance & Traceability

- Maintain batch integrity throughout all stages
- Prevent strain mixing
- Track material from grinding through packaging
- Label all containers and products with strain name and harvest date
- Apply Metrc labels as required
- Maintain production logs and batch records

## Safety

- Wear required PPE at all stages (gloves, hairnet, beard net/mask)
- Keep hands clear of moving machine parts
- Power down machine before adjustments
- Report mechanical issues immediately
- Handle isopropyl alcohol in well-ventilated areas
