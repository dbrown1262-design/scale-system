# Trimming Machine Operation SOP

## 1. Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the proper operation, cleaning, and documentation procedures for the trimming machine used during post-harvest cannabis processing. The purpose of this SOP is to ensure consistent trimming quality, maintain product integrity, prevent cross-strain contamination, and comply with regulatory requirements.

---

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to all employees responsible for trimming cannabis flower using the trimming machine within the processing facility.

This procedure covers:

* Machine setup
* Operation of the trimming machine
* Batch handling
* Strain separation
* Cleaning and sanitation
* Recordkeeping

---

## 3. Responsibilities

### Processing Staff

* Operate trimming equipment according to this SOP.
* Maintain separation of strains and batches.
* Ensure accurate weighing and labeling of trimmed product.
* Perform cleaning between strains.

---

## 4. Equipment and Materials

* Trimming machine
* Totes with liners for flower, smalls and trim.
* Sorting screens (½" and ¾")
* Use Weigh Bucked Totes application to record the weight of each finished tote.
* Cleaning supplies (food-grade alcohol, cloths, brushes)
* Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

---

## 5. Safety Requirements

* Keep hands clear of moving parts.
* Ensure machine guards are properly installed.
* Disconnect power before performing cleaning or maintenance.
* Report equipment malfunctions immediately.

---

## 6. Procedure

### 6.1 Pre-Operation Setup

1. Verify the trimming machine is clean and free of plant material from previous batches.
2. Confirm the machine is properly assembled.
3. Ensure all brushes and components are installed correctly.
4. Inspect blades and confirm they are free of debris.
5. Place collection bins beneath the machine for trim.
6. Confirm the correct strain is being processed.

---

### 6.2 Pre-Trim Processing

1. Retrieve Bucked Flower tote from store room.
2. Inspect flower for foreign material.
3. Load flower into the trimming machine.
4. Make sure the spacer is installed under the brush assembly

Batch size may vary depending on strain characteristics.

General guidelines:

* Use **smaller batches** for large buds or strains with large fan leaves.
* Use **larger batches** for smaller or denser buds.

Do not overload the trimming machine.

---

### 6.3 Machine Operation

1. Turn on the trimming machine.
2. Allow the machine to run for the required trimming interval.
3. Monitor the trimming process to prevent over-trimming or product damage.
4. Remove flower after the initial trimming cycle.

---

### 6.4 Sorting Process

After the initial trimming pass:

1. Pass trimmed flower through a **½ inch sorting screen**.
2. Separate material into:

* **Large flower (bigs)** for manual finishing
* **Small buds (smalls)** for secondary trimming

For strains with larger or fluffier flower structure:

* Use a **¾ inch screen** during the first sorting step.

---

### 6.5 Secondary Trimming

1. Load small buds into the trimming machine for a second trimming pass.
2. Run the machine without spacers for large flower.
3. After trimming, pass the material through the **½ inch screen again** to separate final small buds.

Smalls should be designated for Pre-roll production

---

### 6.6 Manual Finishing

Large buds removed during sorting are manually trimmed by staff to remove remaining sugar leaves and improve product appearance.

Manual trimming output is recorded according to trimming productivity procedures.

---

## 7. Weighing and Identification

Trimmed flower is placed into collection buckets and weighed.

Each bucket is assigned an identification label containing:

* Internal bag number
* Strain name
* Weight

This information is recorded in the facility database.

Labels are used by staff to identify product during further processing.

---

## 8. Strain Separation

To prevent cross-contamination:

1. Only **one strain may be processed at a time**.
2. All trimming equipment must be cleaned between strains.
3. Trim and flower from different strains must never be mixed.

---

## 9. Cleaning and Sanitation

Cleaning must occur between strains.

### Cleaning Steps

1. Turn off and unplug the trimming machine.
2. Remove all remaining plant material from the machine.
3. Wipe interior surfaces with food-grade alcohol.
4. Clean blades and brush assemblies.
5. Remove plant debris from surrounding work areas.
6. Clean all work tables, bins, scissors, and screens.
7. Sweep floors.

The machine must be fully clean before processing the next strain.

---

## 10. Records

The following records must be maintained:

* Trimmer productivity logs
* Bucket weight records

All records must be retained according to facility record retention policies.
