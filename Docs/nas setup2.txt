NAS summary:

we initially installed a ups on the NAS, but it did not have the capacity to run for 8 hours.

We replaced the UPS with an EcoFlow DELTA 2 Power Station.  This unit is designed for longer run times.

We also purchased and Anker SOLIX C300 power station to run our Spectrum modem and router

We implemented a NAS monitor using a Raspberry Pi computer.  The monitor checks the NAS every sixty seconds and updates a (Cloud based) Supabase status table .  If it fails to connect for three consecutive checks, it sends a warning email and logs the event in a Supabase log table.  When the NAS is back up, it sends an email and logs the event.  Finally, the monitor sends a "heartbeat" email every day to confirm the system is running.

We also implemented a log viewer that allows staff to view the current status of the NAS and the message log.

We tested the system by unplugging both power stations for 8 hours.  The EcoFlow unit had 73% of its battery capacity and estimated that it could run an additional 21 hours at the current discharge rate.  The SOLIX power station was still running and had 19% of its battery capacity.

With regards to the requirement for retaining sampling videos, we do not rely on the NAS: we download the video and store it on a google drive, where it can remain until we delete it.
